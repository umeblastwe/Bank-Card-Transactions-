# 💳 Card Transaction Analysis & Customer Insights - Power BI Project

## 📌 Project Overview
An end-to-end FinTech Business Intelligence project analyzing credit card transaction behaviors, spending categories, demographic trends, and customer-level metrics. The project features an interactive multi-page Power BI dashboard designed to uncover actionable consumer insights and spending patterns.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages & Key Insights

### 1. Overview Page (Executive Dashboard)
* **Core Summary Metrics:**
  * **Total Transaction Amount (Sum of Amt):** **73.69M**[cite: 10]
  * **Total Transaction Number:** **1M**[cite: 10]
  * **Average Transaction Amount:** **70.28**[cite: 10]
* **Category Breakdown:** Groceries and shopping lead consumer spending categories, while personal care receives the least attention[cite: 10]. A sharp rise in December reflects increased festival and holiday expenses, indicating strong seasonal trends[cite: 10].
* **Gender Demographics:** 
  * **Female Customers (F):** Account for **54.49%** of total spending, primarily directed toward groceries, shopping, and household needs[cite: 10].
  * **Male Customers (M):** Contribute **45.31%**, with a larger share directed toward travel and entertainment[cite: 10].

### 2. Customer Insights Page
* **Top Spender:** **Jeffery Smith** is identified as the highest-spending customer, with a total spend of approximately **244k**[cite: 9].
* **Age Group Distribution:** 
  * The **60+** segment records the highest overall spending, followed closely by the **31–40** age range[cite: 9].
  * Younger cohorts (ages **21–30** and **under-20**) allocate a larger share of their spending to shopping, whereas older age groups focus more heavily on groceries and essentials[cite: 9].
* **Customer Table:** Detailed view mapping individual customer names, occupations, transaction counts, and average transaction amounts[cite: 9].
